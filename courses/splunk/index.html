Course brief

A production-focused Splunk curriculum for machine-data ingestion, search, dashboards, alerting, distributed architecture, security, and enterprise-scale operations.

This course is organized as a progressive operational curriculum. Lesson files are reserved in the repository structure so future content can be added without changing URLs or navigation.

By the end

You will be able to

  • Ingest, parse, index, normalize, and search machine data effectively
  • Write production SPL for analysis, dashboards, alerts, and operational investigations
  • Design knowledge objects, data models, field extraction, and reusable search content
  • Operate forwarders, indexer clusters, search-head clusters, and distributed deployments
  • Secure, automate, monitor, scale, upgrade, back up, and recover Splunk Enterprise

Complete syllabus

50 chapters in prerequisite order.

The structure goes beyond a service catalog and includes architecture, identity, security, networking, delivery, observability, reliability, governance, automation, cost, troubleshooting, and production operations.
